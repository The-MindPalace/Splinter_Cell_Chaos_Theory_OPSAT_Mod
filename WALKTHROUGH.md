# OPSAT walkthrough dataset

What the OPSAT NEXT card and DVORAK read from: for every objective in every mission, where it is and the stealth ways through it. Researched once from several full walkthroughs, cross-checked against each other, and written in our own words. The overlay reads `CheatOverlay/walkthrough.json` locally, so showing it costs nothing.

Sources:

## Lighthouse

**Route:** Beach cave and crawl space, then the creaky bridge, the barrel room, the torture room, the two courtyards, the first tower (fireplace), the second tower (server), the cannon terrace, then down to the lighthouse.

### Rescue Morgenholt

*Where:* Torture room, up the stairs from the long barrel room.

1. Shoot the light at the top of the wet ramp, OCP the torture-room light and hide in the rubble niche on the left. The guards go up to look and you crouch in.
2. Or wait in the dark: one guard walks up the ramp to the toilets, the other sits in the side office. Slip past or grab the seated one from behind.
3. He can't be saved. Reaching him moves the mission on. Leave through the alcove at the back by the spotlight, then pick the courtyard door lock (don't break it).

### Recover or destroy information about the Masse Kernels

*Where:* Server computer in the second tower, past the courtyards and the fireplace room.

1. Short way: climb the big crate by the second tent, shimmy up the wall, sneak past the two resting by the fireplace and along the upper walkway.
2. Low way: through the doorway under the bright light, past the tin-roof gazebo, then jump onto the roof and OCP the light over the far doorway to slip in.
3. In the tower, grab and KO the man at the computer from behind, or ignore him, and use the computer. That wipes the data.

### Recover or destroy information about the Mass Kernels

*Where:* Server computer in the second tower.

1. Climb the crate by the second tent and take the upper ledge past the fireplace room to the tower.
2. Use the computer. The man at it can be grabbed from behind or left alone.

### Discover where the guerillas got their arms and equipment.

*Where:* Lighthouse ground floor: the radio room just inside the back door.

1. Open the door slowly and let the radio operator talk. Grim picks the answer out of his call. Do not drop him before that.
2. If he is down or saw you first, go up the first flight of stairs and search the small file cabinet.
3. Outside guard: OCP the bulb on the lighthouse wall when he is not looking, then follow him round to the door.

### Disable the guerilla's communications equipment.

*Where:* Radio set in the lighthouse ground-floor room.

1. After Grim's call about the Maria Narcissa, OCP the room light and grab the operator, or creep up behind him while he sits.
2. Then shoot the radio with the pistol, or throw the bottle beside it.
3. Door-bash: stay outside the closed door, thermal on, whistle, and bash it as he reaches it. Save first.

### Disable the guerilla's communications equipment.

*Where:* Radio set in the lighthouse ground-floor room.

1. Take the operator quietly (OCP his light or grab him seated), then shoot or bottle the radio.

### Scan the SSCC Bar Code of the delivered crates.

*Where:* Five gun crates along the route. EEV scans from cover.

1. 1 by the generator in the long barrel room. 2 in the target-practice room downstairs (scan it from the ladder dark).
2. 3 inside the second courtyard tent: cut the back cloth to get in. 4 under the tin-roof gazebo: scan it from the upper walkway.
3. 5 on the cannon terrace below the second tower: turn right among the cannons while the two guards talk.

### Eliminate Hugo Lacerda

*Where:* Not here. He left by boat an hour ago (the tent guard tells you).

1. Cancelled in this mission. He is on the Maria Narcissa next.

### Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point

*Where:* Top of the lighthouse: lamp gallery.

1. Climb the ladder while the gallery guard has his back to you, flip the switch by the door to kill the lamp, then call extraction.
2. Or switch it off and wait inside. He comes back to relight it, so KO him from behind.
3. Or tag him from the ladder with a sticky shocker or airfoil round.

*Watch:* At the terrace stairs a guard may walk down to the cannon. Shoot the lamp on the left and let him settle facing away.

## Cargo Ship

**Route:** Fore deck, cargo hold, woodshop, pump room (drain), flooded hold (bill), engine room, crew quarters, ship office (ledger), outside stairs, bridge (captain), captain cabin (Lacerda), stern launch.

### Retrieve the bill of lading for Lacerda's arms shipment.

*Where:* The flooded hold off the lower corridor. Drain it first from the auxiliary pump room.

1. Drain: KO the woodshop worker, take the high crawl space. Hop the right rail onto the pipes, OCP the swinging lamp, and use the console while the tech walks off.
2. Or clear the pump room: shoot the walkway bulb from the dark corner, KO the walkway guard back at his post, then take the tech away from the ladder.
3. Then in the drained hold, climb the crates and scan the high crate with the red-tagged bill. The paper on the floor-level crate isn't it.
4. Two guards come down the corridor after. Duck into the open vent under the walkway and let them pass.

### Retrieve the ship's transit ledger for Lacerda's shipment.

*Where:* Ship office, up the stairs from the crew quarters (past the engine room).

1. Easy: take the crawl space by the office doorway, stay dark while the two talk, and EEV-scan the hard drive (the tower, not the screen).
2. Or kill the first room light at its switch, OCP the inner light. When the clerk gets up, KO him and use his computer or the file cabinet.
3. Shoot the bulb outside the door first. The guard who checks it can be taken when he leans on the rail.

*Watch:* Engine room: use the OCP only, no bullets. Jump-and-hide on the catwalk rails to pass the two guards.

### Find out which cabin Lacerda is in.

*Where:* The bridge, through the Deck 4 door at the top of the outside stairs.

1. Open the Deck 4 door and step back down while they notice it. Then grab the helmsman and drag him out before the KO.
2. Grab the captain from behind and interrogate him. He gives you the cabin.
3. Or read the ship register in the computer room. From the Deck 4 door, hang below the rail and shimmy right, then go in the lit door and KO the man in the chair.

### Find out which cabin Lacerda is in

*Where:* The bridge (Deck 4).

1. Interrogate the captain on the bridge, or read the register in the computer room.

### Eliminate Hugo Lacerda.

*Where:* Captain's cabin: down the bridge stairs, past the cabin with the loud music, right at the T, end on the right.

1. Wait outside the kitchen door. After his talk he comes in for a drink. Grab him there; his guards will not notice.
2. Back out of the cabin and down the stairs before you finish him.
3. Interrogate him several times first for the bonus.

### Interrogate Hugo Lacerda

*Where:* Captain's cabin kitchen.

1. Grab him when he comes for a drink and interrogate him repeatedly before you finish him.

### Find the name of Lacerda's Panamanian contact.

*Where:* Overheard in Lacerda's cabin.

1. Stand in the cabin hall by the closed door and listen to the whole conversation before you move in.
2. Missed it? There's a second chance in the Bank: the small computer room by the safety deposit boxes.

### Place tracking devices on any illegal weapons crates.

*Where:* Six crates along the route.

1. 1 in the first crate hold, just through the squeeze gap. 2 in a closet in the crew quarters past the engine room.
2. 3 at the foot of the outside stairs from the ship office. 4 on Deck 3 near the stairs down by the captain cabin.
3. 5 in the passage behind the Deck 2 galley: follow the guard who walks to the freezer, OCP the nearest light, tag it, get out.
4. 6 on the lower stern deck (three guards). Do it on the way to extraction: rope ladder near the Deck 5 ladder.

### Exfiltrate to the extraction point.

*Where:* The ship's launch at the stern, top level.

1. Leave the bridge by the door near the stairs, past the ladder, up the stairs to the ladder down. That skips the three Deck 5 guards.
2. At the bottom, ignore the stern guards. Climb the short ladder and take the extraction prompt (weapon holstered).
3. Want crate 6? Sticky-camera gas the two talkers from the walkway first, then the third with an airfoil round.

## Bank

**Route:** Front courtyard, right alley ladder, roof shed, skylight rope into the lobby lasers, then the ground floor (retinal door, treasurer, rear courtyard) and the second floor (laser rooms, security office, president), the three vault panels, the deposit box, the vault, and back out over the front wall.

**Codes:** Ground-floor security booths 3624. Treasurer 8645. 2F security office 2306. President 3490. Rear courtyard stair door 3901. Deposit box 1024.

### Enter the Bank.

*Where:* Front courtyard, then the roof skylight (or the front doors).

1. Walk on the concrete; the grass has motion sensors. The breaker down the left alley kills the courtyard lights.
2. Roof: go down the right alley, OCP the high wall camera, jump to the ladder left of the fire exit. Break the shed lock, use the left computer to open a dome window, rope down.
3. Front doors: pick the lock in the dark, OCP the camera over the desk, KO the desk guard. Only reaches reception if the glass doors are shut.
4. On the rope: EEV one of the glowing cubicle computers and pick 'Deactivate Laser Grid'.

### Gain entry to the Bank via the rooftops, or through the front entrance.

*Where:* Roof skylight or front doors.

1. Right alley ladder to the roof, shed switch opens the dome, rope down; EEV a cubicle PC to drop the lasers.

### Authorize the vault access from three officers' panels.

*Where:* Panels in the Treasurer's office (ground floor east), the 2F security office and the President's office.

1. Treasurer: through the retinal door (stand up, grab the sleeping guard, make him scan, or hack it). Code 8645, or let the keypad guard say it. Panel is under the painting, back left.
2. Security office (2F): code 2306, or the secret way: rear courtyard pipe, balcony crawl space, EEV the computer tower to stop the fan, drop in. Panel by the door.
3. President: code 3490 (overheard in the rear courtyard) or hack it. Panel by the desk.
4. Back down fast: out the President's window ledge to ledge. The crawl space under the courtyard camera leads into the Treasurer's office.

### Look for any suspicious transaction records.

*Where:* File cabinet beside the desk in the Treasurer's office (ground floor east).

1. Door code 8645, or listen to the guard working the keypad and use it after he leaves.
2. Or crawl in from the rear courtyard through the wall vent. OCP the camera above it first.
3. Search the cabinet next to the desk.

### Plant a series of false emails to make it look like an inside job.

*Where:* Eight computers: reception, two ground-floor booths, the 2F laser-room desk, the three panel offices, the vault.

1. Reception: EEV the PC on the floor left of the desk from the dark. Both ground-floor booth PCs can be EEV-hacked through the windows (door code 3624).
2. Upstairs: the desk in the first laser room, the security office, the President's office. Then the Treasurer's office.
3. The vault computer finishes the set. Save before each hack; the EEV gives you less time than the keyboard.

### Delete the camera archives for the bank.

*Where:* 2F glass security office.

1. Regular: past the laser rooms (carry the KO-d laser guard through the beams), code 2306, then wait for both to sit and drag them out one at a time.
2. Secret: rear courtyard pipe, then the balcony crawl space over the fan room. EEV the tower to stop the fan, sticky-camera gas both guards, drop in.
3. At the computer: disable the cameras and delete the archives.

### Recover additional safecracking equipment from the small vault

*Where:* Safety deposit room, through the glass door by the three-man booth (it opens once all three panels are done).

1. Box 1024. Run along the rows until the open prompt shows, then take the gear.
2. From upstairs, drop out of the President's window instead of the laser rooms. The glass door is near the Treasurer's office.

### Use the telemetric lock pick to bypass the double lock.

*Where:* Main vault door, down the stairs past the deposit room.

1. Place the telemetric pick in one side lock, then lockpick the other.
2. Pause after each tumbler and continue only when its light turns green.

### Plant the releasing charge on the main vault door.

*Where:* The main vault door wheel.

1. Place the charge on the centre of the door, back off until Detonate appears.
2. Going in: OCP the camera at the far end of the vault.

### Steal $50,000,000 in French Governmental Bearer Bonds.

*Where:* Cage at the back of the vault.

1. Vault still shut? Box 1024 in the deposit room first, then the telemetric pick and a lockpick on the two door locks, then the charge on the wheel.
2. Vault computer: open the cage, take the bonds. Lights-off and sprinklers are there if guards come.
3. Leaving: thermal on and OCP one emitter of the beam across the vault door, then walk out before it returns.
4. Or carry a guard's body through the beam. That has to be done before you pick up the bonds.

### Determine who MCAS Bank purchased the arms for.

*Where:* Computer at the back of the main vault (the door opens with the gear from deposit box 1024).

1. Vault still shut? Get box 1024 in the deposit room first (glass door by the three-man booth): the lock pick and the charge.
2. At the door: telemetric pick in one side lock, lockpick the other, pausing on each tumbler until green. Charge on the wheel, detonate from a distance.
3. Inside: OCP the camera at the far end, then use the computer at the back. Hack it too for the email set.

### Find the name of Lacerda's Panamanian contact.

*Where:* Small room by the safety deposit box room entrance.

1. Use the computer in the little room next to the deposit boxes.

### Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point.

*Where:* Front wall where you started.

1. Leave by the EXIT door across from the closet on the ground floor, run the alley to the courtyard, and take the 'exfiltrate' prompt at the wall right of the gates.
2. Guards still up front? Climb the pipe right of the front doors and zip across the courtyard.

### Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point.

*Where:* Front wall where you started.

1. Fire exit, alley, courtyard wall right of the gates. The pipe and zip line right of the front doors skips the guards.

## Penthouse

**Route:** Alley and street, apartment building elevator, roof helipad, AXE sign cables, penthouse lower balcony, kitchen and napping guard, outside stairs, guesthouse (music guard), patio, main floor, upstairs server, greenhouse, then across the sniper roof to Dvorak, and back to the stuck elevator.

**Codes:** Dvorak's door 0280 (also on Zherkezhi's hidden computer).

### Get to the top of the building being used by the National Guard.

*Where:* Apartment building across the street, up to its roof.

1. Street: from behind the dumpster, shoot out the big spotlight, then OCP the second one and slip in the lit doorway on the right.
2. High road: the ladder by the trash bags, up the fire escape, jump from the empty box to the ledge, shimmy left to the pipe and climb down by the door.
3. Inside, wait for the tech to fix the elevator and ride up. Or slip in while he's at his toolbox, climb onto the car roof and take the long ladder (crouch as the car rises).
4. Top floor: thermal-check the guard, OCP the light outside the elevator and the EXIT sign, take the stairs to the roof.

### Get to Zherkezhi's penthouse in the adjacent building.

*Where:* Across the roof to the AXE sign, cables down to the penthouse balcony.

1. Flip the light switch to the left to pull the helipad guards away. OCP the chopper spotlight, mantle the pipes, then OCP the AXE sign and climb the ladder beside it.
2. Or go straight over the pipes across from the exit and stay dark, away from the helicopter.
3. At the top, jump for the cables and slide over. On the penthouse ledge hang off the edge and shimmy right past the balcony guard, then climb up behind him.

### Get to Zherkezhi's penthouse in the adjacent building.

*Where:* Across the roof to the AXE sign, cables down to the penthouse balcony.

1. Light switch on the left decoys the helipad guards. OCP the AXE sign, climb its ladder, take the cables, shimmy the ledge past the balcony guard.

### Find out who the mercenaries work for.

*Where:* Any of the penthouse guards.

1. Grab and interrogate the lower balcony guard once you climb up behind him.
2. Or the guard napping by the computer past the kitchen (grab him standing).
3. Or just listen: the two guards meeting on the patio talk about it.

### Find the architectural plans.

*Where:* Computer next to the napping guard, lower floor past the unfinished kitchen.

1. From the elevator room, while the tech and guard argue, cut the hanging sheet by the balcony door, take the high crawl space, cross the kitchen.
2. Hack the computer beside the sleeping guard. Then pick the locked door out.

### Find the architectural plans.

*Where:* Computer next to the napping guard, lower floor.

1. Cut the sheet by the balcony door, crawl space, kitchen, then hack the computer by the sleeping guard.

### Tap the Penthouse video cameras.

*Where:* Six infrared cameras (night vision shows the beams). Walk under each and tap its cord.

1. 1: outside the napping guard room, at the foot of the stairs (OCP it, then climb). 2: the patio, behind the tree; the switch right of the glass door kills the lights.
2. 3 and 4: the living room (one in the small room on the right). 5: upstairs in the room with the computer screens.
3. 6: just left of the greenhouse door. The most often missed one.

### Access Zherkezhi's Server to determine who Dvorak is.

*Where:* Zherkezhi's hidden room upstairs: through the doorway left of the camera wall, then left down a narrow corridor.

1. Kitchen guard: from the crawl-space nook, OCP the hanging light and take him. Pillar guard: OCP the hall light and whistle him over.
2. OCP the stair camera, go up, slip under the room camera. Find the hidden door (walk along the closets / behind the bed) to the small room.
3. Use that computer. It names Dvorak and gives door code 0280.

### Discover who is in charge of the Zherkezhi protection detail.

*Where:* Greenhouse guard, upper floor.

1. Optic-cable the greenhouse door and let the talk finish. One guard comes out, so hide in the corner past the camera.
2. He stops to stare out of the big open window. Grab him there and interrogate him.

### Locate Dvorak.

*Where:* The abandoned building across the sniper roof: through the guesthouse, then the door beside it.

1. Back down the kitchen crawl space to the patio. OCP the music guard's light, slip round the wall, then take the door next to the guesthouse.
2. On the roof: climb the pipe by the door and zip to the caged generator, switch it off. Or shoot the light over the far door. Snipers sit in the high windows.
3. Next door: thermal, whistle, door-bash the guard. Left is the keypad (0280, or hack). Right is the sniper nest: disarm the wall mine on green, take the crawl space.

### Retrieve a hardcopy output for a single Dvorak execution cycle.

*Where:* Dvorak's machine room.

1. Talk to the old man until he takes you to the machine, then again to get his help.
2. Start the main server. Flip each of the three banks on the left as it lights (same order every time).
3. Collect the output by the overhead light on the far side.

### Obtain the missing transaction records.

*Where:* Upstairs computer on the table in the camera-swept room.

1. OCP the room camera, cross, use the computer. Only needed if you missed the Treasurer cabinet in the Bank.

### Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point.

*Where:* The stuck elevator car between the penthouse floors.

1. Back across the sniper roof while the patroller is away, through the guesthouse (light switch decoy), patio, kitchen crawl space.
2. OCP the camera by the elevator nook, hop the metal beam onto the car roof, drop in and extract.
3. Or the living-room bathroom crawl space comes out at the elevator.

## Displace

**Route:** Roof fan control, fan shaft rappel, vents over the meeting room, Operations Room, keypad doors to the lower floor, Level 2 lobby, training gym, R&D (lasers / break-room crawl space), vents to the main server room, elevator to Level 3, executive wing, Nedich office, fire stairs.

**Codes:** Exec-wing doors from Operations (lounge guard) 8136. Level 3 side door 2346. Nedich office 2609. Fire door 3485.

### Don't kill anyone.

*Where:* Whole mission.

1. Any death fails the mission. Knock out, never kill. Watch drops from heights, and do not leave KO-d guards where they can fall.

### Retrieve the server access algorithm.

*Where:* The executive's laptop: the meeting room, then wherever the execs walk next.

1. From the vent over the meeting room, EEV the laptop under the glass table through the closed trap door and hack it fast while they talk.
2. Lower floor: through the keypad door (8136), mantle over the rail by the LEVEL 1 sign and wait at the far end. EEV the laptop when he sets it down (stand, not crouched).
3. Easiest: the same data sits on the gym computer at the back of the training gym (the tracer server). One hack does both.
4. Upper door: hide behind it while one exec clears the window, then sticky-camera gas both execs and use the laptop.

### Insert tracer programs into Displace's division servers

*Where:* Three division servers.

1. 1: Operations Room, the computer alone on the table on its top tier.
2. 2: training gym, the computer at the back of the computer half. Shimmy the overhead pipe to it.
3. 3: upstairs in R&D behind a keypad. Hack the keypad, or take the vent from the back of the turret range.

### Discover who is in charge of the Zherkezhi protection detail.

*Where:* Operations Room, by the meeting room.

1. EEV-hack the rightmost computer against the back wall from the dark. It names Milan Nedich.

### Recover technical reports into ongoing Displace turret research

*Where:* R&D main room, lower floor: the left computer on the table by the windows.

1. Into R&D: hack the retinal scanner and carry a KO-d guard through the lasers.
2. Or the crawl space at the far end of the gym locker room into the R&D break room. Wait for the gamers to finish talking.
3. EEV-hack the computer from the shadows if workers are still about.

### Recover technical specifications for the Infrasound Generator

*Where:* Not covered by any PC walkthrough. Likely unused in this build.

1. If it is live, check the R&D computers on the way to the server room.

### Access the Central Server in the server room

*Where:* Main server room, reached through the R&D vents.

1. R&D upstairs: grab the worker at his computer (or wait him out), hack the keypad room and take its crawl space down to the rope.
2. Or the vent at the back of the turret range. OCP the room first.
3. Rope down, start the server with the algorithm and read it. Then climb straight back up before the lights come on.

*Watch:* Have the algorithm before you go.

### Discover the real identity of Milan Nedich.

*Where:* Level 3: the room with three frosted glass panels by its door.

1. Stand at a frosted panel and point the EEV at it until it reads 'Recording'. Stay for the whole talk.
2. Do not OCP the panels and do not just optic-cable it; neither counts.
3. Missed it? Hokkaido gives a second chance: scan the car plates.

### Find information linking Displace and Zherkhezi

*Where:* Milan Nedich's office in the executive wing on Level 3.

1. Elevator to Level 3 (from the Level 2 lobby, or the server-room shortcut to Level 1). Two guards in the hall say a door code; sticky-camera gas them after.
2. OCP the camera at the side-hall door, code 2346. The occupied office: whistle and door-bash, or interrogate him for the next code.
3. Nedich office: code 2609 or hack it. Use the computer nearest the door.

### Find information linking Displace and Zherkhezi

*Where:* Nedich's office, Level 3 executive wing.

1. Side-hall door 2346 (OCP the camera), Nedich office 2609; the computer nearest the door.

### Exfiltrate from the primary extraction point.

*Where:* Fire stairs off the dark niche past the camera door on Level 3.

1. Back through the camera door (OCP it), find the fire door in the dark niche, code 3485 (from Nedich's computer), climb to the top.

### Exfiltrate from the primary extraction point.

*Where:* Fire stairs, Level 3.

1. Camera door, dark niche, fire door 3485, up the stairs.

## Hokkaido

**Route:** Lake shore and rock-perch guard, small dojo, car courtyard, the big building (porch window or under the walkway), sauna and hot-coals rooms, dining area, practice yard, Buddha room, Nedich in the bathhouse, terrarium, TV room, glass-door house, broom-closet crawl space, teahouse roof.

### Discover the real identity of Milan Nedich

*Where:* The two cars in the courtyard past the first dojo.

1. Blow out the candle inside the dojo. Both car guards come in to look, so slip out the door or window and scan both plates.
2. Or EEV-scan the plates from the dark.
3. Or sticky-camera gas the two by the car and scan at leisure. Drag them into the dark alley right of the next building.

### Retrieve the hidden microphones

*Where:* Six mics behind scrolls or by the phones. Thermal or EMF shows them.

1. 1: big building, the mic hunter's room by the plant near the light. 2: dining area, wall right of the phone (the bathroom left crawl space to the kitchen gets you round).
2. 3: the bedroom beside Nedich's bathhouse room, corner by the phone. 4: TV room, wall left of the phone.
3. 5: the bedroom off the TV room, left of the bed by the door. 6: downstairs in the two-floor house past the glass door (the art studio), left of the big bed.

### Eliminate Milan Nedich

*Where:* Bathhouse room off the hall from the golden Buddha.

1. Do not use the door. Take the floor crawl space to its right and wait in the corner.
2. Nedich crosses to the tub room. Take the guard who stops inside the doorway (from behind, or an airfoil round as he turns).
3. Shut the door, then grab Nedich through the paper wall where you see his shadow (weapon away). Back into the dark corner, interrogate, finish.

*Watch:* Leave him alive and he turns up later in the car courtyard with guards.

### Interrogate Milan Nedich

*Where:* Bathhouse, through the paper wall.

1. Grab him through the paper wall and interrogate him before you finish him.

### Record the conversation between Shetland and Zherkezhi

*Where:* Teahouse: listen from the little roof across from it.

1. TV room: OCP the TV and take the guard who comes to it. Then the glass-door house: kill the lights, take the two outside guards with non-lethal rounds.
2. Upstairs broom closet with the curtain: the crawl space under the medkit puts you on the roof facing the teahouse.
3. Stay on that roof and EEV the teahouse for the whole conversation.

### Abduct Abrahim Zherkezhi

*Where:* Teahouse.

1. Scripted: Shetland kills him during the meeting. Recording that meeting is what counts.

### Eliminate Douglas Shetland

*Where:* Teahouse, then the alley gate.

1. Scripted escape. Sticky-shock the guard below, drop down, through the teahouse sliding door near the left end.
2. Go through the crouch hole and down the alley to the gate. He gets away by helicopter regardless.

### Exfiltrate to the primary extraction point

*Where:* The lake shore where you started.

1. From the alley, shoot out the lantern by the fence gate, then the one outside the bright dojo. Slip through the dojo.
2. Out the far side, down the rocks to the shore.

*Watch:* Three new guards wait in the car courtyard on the way back.

## Battery

**Route:** Rope and trap door into the warehouse, security office and offices, bunk room, munitions warehouse, missile loading bay (ride the missile beam), tunnel to the elevator between the two bay control rooms, command center up top, West launcher (BIOS), then the East launcher (abort).

### Recover the shipping and repair logs.

*Where:* Four computers along the route.

1. 1: office area past the first warehouse, the enclosed side office. Kill the lights, grab the man at it, or EEV it through the blinds.
2. 2: the locked security office by the munitions warehouse stairs. Pick it and EEV the hard drive past the music listener.
3. 3: upper floor of the command center. 4: the control room under the West launcher (the computer the pacing guard sits at).

### Determine if the Koreans intentionally launched the missile.

*Where:* Command center at the top of the elevator.

1. Secret route: left of the elevator doors, shimmy up into the ceiling gap, bypass the panel on the car roof. From the fan room, the hidden ladder drops you behind the map displays.
2. Or hack the elevator keypad, hide behind the boxes (OCP the car light). Follow the guard through the retinal doors after OCP-ing the camera.
3. Listen to the commander and his men without interrupting. Missed it? A computer on the lower floor has it.

### Determine North Koreas divisional strength down the Kaesong-Munsan approach.

*Where:* Base commander's office, command center upper floor.

1. Take the security-room guard in the hall, the upper-floor watcher, then the seated man below.
2. Whistle from his doorway and step back down the stairs, then grab him when he turns. Or OCP his computer and shoot his light; he walks the whole floor.
3. Interrogate, then KO.

### Prime a missile in each of the missile bays.
